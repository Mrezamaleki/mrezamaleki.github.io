Developed sparse SOCP verification using selective McCormick lifting and local SOC constraints. The CORE class-wise cascade matched SDP-certified robust accuracy with up to 86.30% lower average verification time on a fully connected MNIST benchmark. The related sparse-relaxation manuscript is under review at AISTATS 2027.

CoRe-CROWN combines a Dual-CROWN training objective with sparse SOCP-derived regularization and influence-based block selection. This manuscript is under review at AAAI 2027.

Presented **CEAR: Certified Ensemble Adversarial Robustness in DNNs** at the Canadian Conference on Artificial Intelligence, held at Simon Fraser University in May 2026. The paper develops an ensemble approach that combines diverse noise-trained networks, voting rules, and randomized smoothing to improve certified adversarial robustness.
